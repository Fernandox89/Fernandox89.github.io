What is HTML?

HTML is like the skeleton of a website. It tells the browser what things exist like text, images or buttons.

Reference: https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML

Head and Body

The head is where you put info like title or styles. The body is everything you see on the page.

Reference: https://www.w3schools.com/html/html_intro.asp
